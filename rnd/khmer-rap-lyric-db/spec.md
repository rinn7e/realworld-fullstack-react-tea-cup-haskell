## 3. Unique Challenges & How to Solve Them

While this project is incredibly exciting, we must plan for a few specific hurdles:

### A. The Speed of Delivery (Flow and Slang)

* **The Challenge:** Rap music is fast, and transcribing complex bars or rapid-fire verses accurately can be incredibly difficult, especially with heavy street slang.
* **The Solution:** Build a **Crowdsourced Submission System** (similar to the Kun Khmer project). Let the community submit transcriptions, and implement a "Verified Lyricist" badge for the actual artists or elite fans who can correct mistakes.

### B. Intellectual Property & Copyright

* **The Challenge:** Lyrics are technically the intellectual property of the songwriters and music labels (like Baramey or Klapyahandz).
* **The Solution:** Lyric websites generally fall under fair use for educational and archival purposes, provided they do not sell the text itself. To keep you completely safe, we will include a prominent **DMCA/Copyright Takedown Link** in the footer and clearly credit the official copyright owners, linking back to their official streaming platforms.
